## Tier 1 — Job boards (unbeatable on vacancy head terms)
- **Indeed.nl** (Recruit Holdings): carriergids salary pages (wat-verdient-een-elektricien; career/elektricien/salaries/Rotterdam with company-level pay, 1.3k reports) + infinite vacancy pages. Strengths: domain authority, city×role long tail, self-reported salary data. **Gaps:** self-report samples only (no structured offer data), no reverse-marketplace offering, low-craft boilerplate around salary rows.
- **Nationale Vacaturebank**: /vacatures/functie/elektricien (208 vacancies, 190 "vast" = permanent → ranks on permanent demand, NOT zzp/flex — the ZZP vacancy SERP is thinner); /salaris/elektricien (€3.459/mo, employer-level bands, best-payers list). Runs Intermediair on the same salary template.
- **Exploitable gap (both):** no transparent offer-level data, no "employers compete for you" narrative, city salary pages limited to major cities.

## Tier 2 — Specialist staffing firms (strong on employer "inhuren" terms, weak content)
- **MAATT** (maatt.nl): siloed werkgever/werknemer architecture, VCU/NEN trust claims, own training centre. Audited 2026-11-05: thin/templated content, **no salary data, no case studies**.
- **PIT** (pitbv.nl): **only player publishing hourly rate bands per level** (starter €15-18 → projectleider €32-45) — partial answer to "wat kost een elektromonteur". SNAb-certified, 24-48u placement promise.
- Others: Talent4s/Electric4s (Enschede), FlexHero (Eindhoven), Proton Group (Eindhoven), JJM Personeel (Brabant, international workers, VCA/NEN/ABU/NEN 4400-1 compliance), i-u.nl (Apeldoorn/Zwolle, ABU/VCU/NEN 4400-1). Big agencies (Hays/YER) not seen ranking on electrician terms in result sets (ASSUMPTION they rank on generic "technisch recruitment" — untested).
- **Common gap:** all sell hours of people; none competes on price transparency, comparison or worker leverage; conversion-only pages, no data content.

## Tier 3 — ZZP tech platforms (closest conceptual competitors)
- **Tech Matchers** (techmatchers.nl): B2B zzp platform elektrotechniek/installatietechniek; +1,250 zzp'ers; 24h payout (Finqle); "up to 64% cheaper than traditional recruitment"; small blog (DBA). **Strongest content-adjacent player in the niche found.**
- **Techers** (techers.nl): zzp coupling elektro/installatietechniek, zakelijke opdrachten model.
- **YouLinked** (youlinked.nl): consumer/B2B klussen for electricians, 9% commission.
- **Hollandworx** (hollandworx.nl): app-based bouw/techniek zzp, includes invoicing.
- **FixThatNow** (fixthatnow.nl): free/no-commission, since July 2025, ~999 signed tradespeople, "nog in opbouw".
- **elektriciensgezocht.nl** (Afbouw Groep, Breda): exact-match domain on "elektricien gezocht"; thin single lead-gen page, no blog; rides parent-domain authority.
- **Exploitable gap (tier 3 across):** fragmented messaging, no market-data content, no salary transparency, several tiny/young; **none offers anonymous reverse bidding** — that positioning is unoccupied.

## Tier 4 — Horizontal on-demand marketplaces
- **Temper** (temper.works): Amsterdam, since 2015; shift-based horeca/bouw/retail/logistiek; published platform fee €4.40-4.90/uur (go.temper.works/nl-nl/bedrijven/bouw); strong domain + press. **Weak in elektrotechniek** (no electrician vertical pages found). Legal overhang: schijnzelfstandigheid/cassatie ruling — directly relevant to OfferMarket's DBA/Wtt positioning (Temper's "platforms versus uitzendbureaus" page covers it). Comparison-page subject, not a head-term blocker.
- **Werkspot / elektriciensbijmij.nl / FixThatNow / Trustoo**: surface on "vakman vinden" queries (verified 2026-11-05) — consumer repair intent, adjacent not core.

## Tier 5 — Associations, education, aggregators (authority, not commercial competitors)
- **Techniek Nederland** (absorbed Uneto-VNI): verified live in SERP for "vind-een-vakman" — the sector anchor; also the strongest citation target (corroborates ai-search-geo).
- **ROC/SBB ecosystem**: education pages rank on "elektricien opleiding/worden".
- **Beroepen.nl / Werk.nl / UWV**: profession encyclopaedia pages with salary bands; informational, link-rich, non-converting.
- **Aggregators/niche proof-of-model:** Techniekwerkt (609 electrician vacancies), koel-monteur.nl, regiodienst.nl, voort.com/vacatures/installatietechniek — **evidence the per-role hub model works in this SERP niche** (supports KEYWORD_DATABASE cluster H).

## OfferMarket's own detectable position
`site:offermarket.eu` / "offermarket.eu electrician" → **zero pages** (2026-11-05). Sitemap has only 4–9 static routes; no article URLs. No detectable organic visibility for electrician keywords. (Corroborates ai-search-geo's brand-invisibility finding; root causes in AUDIT_REPORT.md F2/F5/F13.)

## Aggregate exploitable gaps (cross-tier)
1. **Structured offer-level salary data** — nobody has it (job boards use self-reports; staffing firms publish nothing; PIT's bands are the only partial exception).
2. **Cost/price-transparency content** — "wat kost elektricien inhuren / vacature plaatsen" answered by nobody with structured data.
3. **ZZP informational space** — conversion-only pages, no content authority anywhere.
4. **Reverse/anonymous bidding positioning** — occupied by no one; Qpromo is the nearest model match (per ai-search-geo).
5. **Per-role hubs** — scattered, few dedicated hubs; proven model in the niche.
